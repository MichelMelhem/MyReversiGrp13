How it works

  • Black always moves first. Against the computer you play Black.
  • Online, colours are drawn at random when you are paired.
  • Legal squares are highlighted on your turn — click one, or type a square like D3.
  • If you have no legal move your turn is skipped automatically.
  • Leaving a game in progress counts as a forfeit. If your connection drops you have 30 seconds to come back.
  • Every finished game is saved — replay it from History and climb the Leaderboard.
