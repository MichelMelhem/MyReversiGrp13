How to play Reversi

Two players, one black and one white, take turns placing discs on the board.

Rules

  1. The game starts with four discs in the centre: two black and two white, placed diagonally.
  2. Black always moves first.
  3. A legal move places a disc on an empty square so that it outflanks one or more opponent discs — traps a straight line of them (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) between the new disc and another disc of your colour.
  4. Every outflanked opponent disc, in every direction, is flipped to your colour.
  5. If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped and your opponent plays again.
  6. The game ends when neither player can move (usually when the board is full). The player with more discs wins; equal counts is a draw.

MyReversi extras

  • Board size: 6×6, 8×8 or 10×10.
  • Board colour: classic green, ocean, wood or slate.
  • Computer opponent: Easy plays random legal moves, Normal prefers corners and edges, Hard searches several moves ahead.
  • Leaving or disconnecting: leaving a game in progress is a forfeit. A disconnected online player has 30 seconds to reconnect before forfeiting.
  • History & leaderboard: every finished game is saved and can be replayed.
