        How to play

        • Black moves first. Players take turns placing one disc.
        • A move must trap at least one line of the opponent's discs between the new disc and another disc of your colour (across, down or diagonal).
        • All trapped discs are flipped to your colour.
        • If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped.
        • The game ends when neither player can move. The player with the most discs wins.
